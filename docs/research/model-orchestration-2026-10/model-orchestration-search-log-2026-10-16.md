Useful conceptual mappings: **architect/editor** is serial proposal→edit; **planner/executor**, **boss/worker**, and **conductor/worker** usually denote hierarchical assignment but do not specify persistence; **council/MoA** may only aggregate answers; **blackboard** means shared state or a task board, not necessarily peer messaging; **swarm/team** may describe durable independent sessions, transient subprocesses, or only prompt roles. Inspect the actual loop and data boundaries before treating these labels as equivalent.

SWE-agent/mini-SWE-agent remain relevant baseline harnesses and are included in the new empirical source; no primary source inspected here established that native heterogeneous multi-worker orchestration is their central supported workflow. They should not be added to a mixed-agent shortlist merely because they support many underlying models.
